How to play

Simple. Sneaky. Last one standing wins.

The goal

Be the last player left alive. Your job is to get your target to accept a specific weapon from you at a specific place.

Example: your weapon is a ketchup bottle and your place is the pool. Hand it to your target at the pool, get them to take it, and you've made your kill.

How it works

  1. Everyone adds ideas

    Before the game starts, everyone adds weapon and location ideas to the pool.

  2. The hunt begins

    With at least 3 players and enough weapons and locations, the organiser starts the game.

  3. You get a secret contract

    You're given a target, a weapon, and a location. Only you know who you're after. The organiser can hand out bonus weapons and locations during the hunt — extra options you can use too.

  4. Make your kill

    Get your target to accept one of your weapons at one of your locations. Any of your assigned or bonus weapons and places will do.

  5. Record it

    When it happens, report the kill on the game page and your target confirms or denies it. If you're the one who got killed, tap “I got killed” and confirm — it's taken at face value, with no confirmation needed from the other player.

  6. Take over the contract

    Once a kill is confirmed, your target is out, and you inherit their contract: their target, and their weapons and locations (bonuses included). Keep going.

Good to know

  • You can only kill your current target, and only with one of your weapons at one of your locations.
  • If a kill is denied, nothing happens.
  • Being out is permanent.
  • Reporting your own death is taken at face value — honesty is assumed.
